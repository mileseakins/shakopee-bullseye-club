SHAKOPEE, MN — The lights are on, the boards are ready, and sixteen competitors are preparing for the inaugural season of the Shakopee Bullseye Club. Beginning Thursday, October 15, eight two-man teams will battle through a double round-robin schedule, with each match featuring A-group 501 singles, B-group 501 singles, and team Cricket.

EIGHT TEAMS, ONE CHAMPIONSHIP
Dave Grandbois and Tony Pastrana; Nick Betsinger and Cole Lair; Brian Dunker and Joe Schuldt; Jason Larson and Jack Stocker; Heang Pao and Brian Pieper; Allex Ege and Stevo Rathsabandith; Nate Carner and Paul Henstein; and Miles Eakins and John Kleingarn make up the inaugural field. With teams assembled for competitive matchups, predicting a favorite before the first dart would be ambitious. Or foolish. Fortunately, this league appears to have no shortage of either quality.

MORE THAN BRAGGING RIGHTS
Alongside the team title, the A and B Group MVP races will reward singles success, while weekly PPR and MPR tracking will reveal the season's standout scorers and biggest improvements. Some players will chase a trophy; others, a personal best. At least one may consider surviving a night without ridicule a victory.

OPENING NIGHT: OCTOBER 15
Eakins and Kleingarn meet Betsinger and Lair, while Ege and Rathsabandith take on Larson and Stocker in the opening 6:30 p.m. session. Four fixtures will kick off the fourteen-week regular season. Nobody can win the title on opening night, but a few players may discover how long a season feels when the darts refuse to cooperate.

THE CHECKOUT
There are no defending champions, no established dynasties, and no excuses left. By February, one team will earn the right to call itself the inaugural Shakopee Bullseye Club champion. The rest will have approximately nine months to explain what went wrong.

The road begins Thursday. Every dart counts.
